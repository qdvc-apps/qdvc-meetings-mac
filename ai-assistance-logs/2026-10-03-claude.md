### The proposal in brief

The structure is the same as the sibling apps:

- **Code:** a Foundation-only `MeetingsCore` library with unit tests, and a SwiftUI/AppKit app on top. There's no Xcode project, and `scripts/build-app.sh` makes an ad-hoc-signed bundle.
- **Storage:** a plain workspace folder, one YAML file per meeting.
- **No network access:** links open in your browser or the relevant app.

**Window.** It's a Mail-style split view.
- **Sidebar:** Today, Upcoming, Past and All, plus a People section that filters meetings by person.
- **Middle:** the meetings, either as a list grouped under date headings or as a month grid like Calendar's Month view. A List / Calendar segmented control in the toolbar switches between them (⌘1 / ⌘2), as Calendar and Finder do.
- **Right:** the meeting pane, laid out like Calendar's event inspector. It shows the title and time, then the location with a Join, Open Map or Show in Maps button, then the platform callout, the people as tokens, the description, and the notes.

**Notes** behave like Reminders: Return adds the next one. Each note's leading symbol shows whether it's a plain note, an action item or a decision. Action items get an "Assigned to" field. It suggests the meeting's people first, then everyone else, then lets you add a new name.

**Exports** are in the toolbar's Share menu and under File → Export Notes: Word, Markdown, and Copy Notes as Plain Text (⇧⌘C). The Word file is generated by the core itself, with no third-party library.

**Two small additions.** Neither is in your brief, so tell me if you'd rather leave them out:
- **Duplicate Meeting (⌘D):** copies a meeting to a week later without its notes, which covers recurring meetings.
- **Rename Person…:** fixes a misspelt name in every meeting at once.

The full detail is in `docs/DESIGN.md`: the location-matching rules, the shortcuts, the file format and the module plan.

### Questions before I start

7. **Action items:** I'd keep them as text with assignees, without a done checkbox, since they're minutes rather than a task list. Say if you want a checkbox.
